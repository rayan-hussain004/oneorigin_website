About OneOrigin

Every product starts from one origin

OneOrigin was founded on a simple idea: organizations shouldn't have to stitch together a dozen disconnected tools just to run their operations. We build focused, enterprise-grade software products — each one deep enough to solve a real problem, all sharing the same foundation of security, reliability and thoughtful design.

Today, that foundation powers two products: OneOps, built for everyday business operations like inventory, billing and GST compliance and OneCampus, built for the specific demands of running educational institutions at scale.

As an MSME-registered, Indian-founded software company, we don't just deliver code—we build lasting partnerships grounded in deep accountability, reliability and long-term commitment.

Why OneCloud

Built for real-world constraints

Cloud infrastructure that adapts to your connectivity, your compliance needs and your workload — not the other way around.

CPU-accelerated compute

Powerful, high-core and high-thread CPU instances built for demanding workloads.

Privacy-first security

Advanced encryption, secure file sharing and powerful access controls to keep your data protected and in your control.

Secure file sharing

Share files with confidence using passwords, verification and advanced access controls.

Automatic backup

Keep your important files protected with automatic backups, file history and recovery when you need it.

Your data stays yours

Built with privacy and control in mind, giving you a secure place to store, manage and share your files.

Storage closer to you

For high-storage enterprise deployments, OneCloud can provide locally positioned infrastructure for faster access, lower latency and offline availability.
